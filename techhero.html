Gabriela Salas Cabrera

Gabriela Salas Cabrera is a programmer and data scientist born in Puerto del Caballo in Chapulhuacán, Hidalgo, Mexico. Her work focuses on the preservation of indigenous languages, and she is most well known for working with Google to add the Náhuatl language to Google Translate...

Gabriela Salas Cabrera (Náhuatl)

Gabriela Salas Cabrera eli se programadora uan se ixtlamatki tlen tlakatki ipan Puerto del Caballo ipan Chapulhuacán, Hidalgo, México. Itequi mosentlalijtok ipan tlamokuitlauilistli tlen tlajtoli tlen indígenas, uan yajaya tlauel kiixmatij pampa tekiti ika Google para kitlalis nopa tlajtoli Náhuatl ipan Google Translate...
