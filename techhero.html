Timnit Gebru

Our Tech Hero — A Champion in Advancing AI Ethics

Timnit Gebru is an Eritrean American computer scientist, activist, and leading voice in the field of AI ethics. She has shaped the tech industry by exposing algorithmic bias and pushing for more diversity and accountability in AI. Her research showed how facial recognition tools often fail on women and people of color, making clear the real-world harms of biased systems.

Gebru also reminds us that ethics in AI is not just about fixing code. It is about changing the structures behind it. She has called for transparency in how data is collected, more representation of marginalized voices, and accountability for the ways AI is used. By founding the Distributed AI Research Institute (DAIR), she created a space to study AI free from corporate influence and pressure.

Through her work and advocacy, Timnit Gebru challenges the tech world to think about not only what AI can do but what it should do.

Our Tech Hero — More than an Athlete

Famous people are often known for one thing that they do, or one industry that they work in. For example, actors and actresses are famous for the movies that they star in. This is similar to professional athletes. However, every now and then we see a professional athlete who makes an impact in another industry, driven by their desire to make a change outside of the world of sports.

On the basketball court, Russell Westbrook is known for being an all-time great; not only has he played in the NBA for over 15 years, but he has won the NBA Most Valuable Player award, amongst other notable accolades. Recently, Russell Westbrook partnered with fellow athletes Kemba Walker and Donnell Beverly Jr. to launch Eazewell. Eazewell is an AI-powered startup that streamlines end-of-life and funeral planning. This algorithm handles things like account closures, documentation, and family communication.

Beverly Jr. and Westbrook got the idea for this program years ago, after the sudden loss of their friend in high school. Not only is this technology providing a great service to families in unfortunate times, but it is showing that people can be influential in multiple spaces, and it is great to be knowledgeable and skilled in different fields.

Our Tech Hero — Preserving Indigenous Languages with AI

Gabriela Salas Cabrera is a programmer and data scientist born in Puerto del Caballo in Chapulhuacán, Hidalgo, Mexico. Her work focuses on the preservation of indigenous languages, and she is most well known for working with Google to add the Náhuatl language to Google Translate. Her work on this project garnered international attention, including landing a spot on the BBC’s 100 Women 2024 list.

She is currently studying data science, artificial intelligence, and natural language processing in Madrid, Spain with the goal of creating a chat GPT system in Náhuatl. Her work spreading awareness of, and preserving indigenous languages is inspiring. Indigenous language loss is a worldwide issue, with many negative consequences: loss of cultural memory, poorer health outcomes and mental health challenges for speakers whose language is threatened, the list goes on. Cabrera’s work is vitally important and harnesses technologies like AI to preserve culture and language, making the world a better place for all of us.
